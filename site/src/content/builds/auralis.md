Auralis opens its meadow gate for one
long midsummer evening of shared food, small ceremonies, and stories carried
between neighbouring worlds.

The landing page keeps the invitation simple: enter the meadow, understand the
place and timing, or reserve your place. Olive, mango, twig lines, and leaf
forms give the page a distinct world and a memorable invitation.
